WhistleDrop: demo sign-ins
==========================

Demo accounts for reviewers to test the moderator and admin features.

Login URL: <LIVE_URL>/mod/login

These credentials are public on purpose. They are used for nothing else.

Sample cases (for /track and the conversation)
----------------------------------------------

Enter a code at <LIVE_URL>/track. Anyone with a code can read that case
and reply in its conversation, exactly as a reporter would.

  WD-DEMO-0001  Security    Under review  Unpatched internal server. Ongoing
                                          conversation; the reporter wrote
                                          last, so it shows as "Awaiting
                                          reply" for moderators.
  WD-DEMO-0002  Corruption  Resolved      Procurement orders split to avoid
                                          a second approval.
  WD-DEMO-0003  Harassment  Closed        Closed case. The whole conversation
                                          stays readable; replying is refused.
  WD-DEMO-0004  Technical   Submitted     Silent backup failures. No activity
                                          yet.
  WD-DEMO-0005  Other       Dismissed     Blocked fire-exit corridor.

All sample content is fictional: no real people or organisations.


Protection
----------

The demo accounts are protected on the server: their role and active status
can't be changed through the API by anyone, and they can't change other
accounts' role or status. They are reset daily: the scheduled cleanup job
makes every demo account active again with its original role.

The demo site runs with DEMO_MODE=true, so the same daily job also puts the
five sample cases back in their original state and deletes any other report
older than 24 hours, with its messages, notes and files. Until then you may
see messages other reviewers have added.

The WD-DEMO codes are deliberately guessable and exist only for the demo.
Please don't enter real personal information anywhere on the demo site.
